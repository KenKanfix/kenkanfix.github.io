Frequently Asked Questions

  • Where is my data stored? All profiles are stored only on your device using local storage. Nothing is sent to any server.
  • How do I delete my data? Remove any profile from within the app, or uninstall the app to erase everything.
  • Why do my calculations differ slightly from another BaZi app? Schools use different conventions for day boundaries and solar terms; this app follows the Hong Kong Observatory lunar calendar data.
