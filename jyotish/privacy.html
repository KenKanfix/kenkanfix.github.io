Life Compass:Jyotish

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Life Compass:Jyotish respects your privacy. This policy explains how your data is handled.

Data Storage

All data you enter (names, birth dates, locations) is stored exclusively on your device using local storage (localStorage). No data is transmitted, collected, or stored on any server.

Location Data

If you use the optional "Use My Location" feature, your current coordinates are used only to pre-fill birth place fields. Location data is never transmitted to any server. The optional city lookup feature queries OpenStreetMap Nominatim directly from your device; no personal data is sent with these requests.

No Tracking

This app does not use cookies, analytics services, or any form of user tracking. No personal information is sent to third parties.

Third-Party Services

This app does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services. All astrological calculations are performed locally using the panchangam-js library.

Data Deletion

You can delete any profile at any time from within the app. To remove all data, clear your browser's local storage or uninstall the app.
