Frequently Asked Questions

  • Where is my data stored? All profiles are stored only on your device using local storage. Nothing is sent to any server.
  • How do I delete my data? Remove any profile from within the app, or uninstall the app to erase everything.
  • Why do my calculations differ slightly from another Jyotish app? This app uses the Lahiri ayanamsa with Udaya tithi calculations via the panchangam-js library, which follows standard Vedic astronomical conventions.
  • What is the difference between North Indian and South Indian chart styles? Both display the same birth chart data. North Indian style fixes houses and rotates signs; South Indian style fixes signs and rotates houses. The choice is purely aesthetic.
  • Does the app require internet? No. All calculations work completely offline. Internet is only used optionally to look up city coordinates when entering a birth place.
